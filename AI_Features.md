# AI Features for ContractIQ

## 1. AI Notification Prioritization
- AI prioritizes notifications based on urgency, due dates, and risk level.
- High-priority notifications are displayed first.

## 2. AI Chatbot for Contracts
- Users can ask questions about contracts using natural language.
- The chatbot provides contract details, obligations, and due dates.

## 3. AI Contract Summarizer
- AI generates a short summary of lengthy contracts.
- It highlights important clauses, obligations, and key dates.

## 4. AI Anomaly Detection
- AI detects unusual activities such as duplicate contracts, unexpected changes, or missing approvals.
- It alerts users when any abnormal activity is found.

## 5. AI Renewal Recommendation
- AI analyzes contract history and performance.
- It recommends whether to renew, renegotiate, or terminate the contract.
